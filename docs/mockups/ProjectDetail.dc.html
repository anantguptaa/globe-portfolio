01

Data

NAIP aerial imagery, with 2,500+ patches I hand-annotated. Results reported on a held-out test set. [Train/test split details.]

02

Model

DINOv3-pretrained ConvNeXt encoders at several sizes, up to convnext_large (~196M parameters), plus DPT decoders with transformer encoders.

03

Training

PyTorch Lightning with a 20-trial Optuna search. Gradient accumulation (effective batch 4 → 16) let the largest encoder train properly.

04

Results

convnext_large rose from 0.8824 to 0.9103 mIoU with the larger batch, overturning an earlier “bigger isn’t better” result. Warmup scheduling made no measurable difference.
